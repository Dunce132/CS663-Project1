Tutorial overview

How does a visual scene become a vibration?

A camera captures image frames from the user’s surroundings and sends them to an embedded processor. Computer-vision algorithms running on the processor analyze the frames to identify objects, estimate their direction, distance, and motion, and evaluate potential risks. The system then converts these visual estimates into haptic signals that communicate information such as direction, proximity, or urgency.
