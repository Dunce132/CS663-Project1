Can You Interpret the Signal?

Use this activity to test your understanding of how direction and distance can be represented through vibration.

Scenario

A wearable device produces a strong, rapidly repeating vibration on the right side. What does this most likely indicate?

Explain the result

Replace this explanation with the reasoning behind the correct answer. For example, motor location can represent direction, while intensity and pulse rate can represent distance or urgency.
